M HANLEY CARTOGRAPHY — REDESIGNED WEBSITE

This version was rebuilt from screenshots of the existing Adobe Portfolio.

To update Cloudflare: unzip the folder, open Workers & Pages > mhanley-cartography > Deployments, upload a new version, and deploy with index.html at the top level.

The portfolio images were extracted from screenshots, so they are lower resolution than the originals. If you provide the original map and logo files later, they can be swapped in without changing the layout.
